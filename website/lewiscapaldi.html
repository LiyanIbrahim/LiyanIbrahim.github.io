Lewis Capaldi — Something in the Heavens

AI Creative · Music Video · Wonder Studios

At Wonder Studios, I worked as AI Creative on Lewis Capaldi's "Something in the Heavens," a surreal, otherworldly visual world that blurs the line between the earthly and the ethereal.

Drawing on themes of love, loss, and transcendence, the project builds an immersive dreamscape where emotion is expressed through environment, with water, light, and nature becoming extensions of the human experience.

I worked at the intersection of generative AI and visual storytelling, shaping the aesthetic direction of the dreamscape, developing AI-driven imagery, and collaborating closely with the creative team to bring the visual world to life.
